## 2. The Pitch: White-Label Quick Scan

### Headline
**"Add AI Security Testing to Your Service Stack — Without Building It"**

### Subheadline
You manage your clients' IT. We test their AI. Same brand. Same trust. Your margin.

### The Offer

| What | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Service** | Fixed-scope AI-security audit (OWASP LLM Top-10, 10 attack classes) |
| **Your cost** | $350 per scan (white-label wholesale) |
| **Your price** | $500–$750 per scan (your retail — you set it) |
| **Your margin** | $150–$400 per scan |
| **Turnaround** | 48 hours from signed authorization |
| **Deliverable** | Branded PDF report (your logo, your cover page) |
| **Upsell path** | $500/mo retainer → $2,000 deep audit (you keep margin on all) |

### How It Works (3 Steps)

1. **You sell it.** Your client needs an AI security check-up. You add it to your next MSA renewal, QBR, or incident response conversation.
2. **We do the work.** Automated OWASP scan + 15-min human triage + scored PDF. Authorized-only (your client signs our template).
3. **You deliver it.** Branded report with your logo. You present findings. You own the relationship.

### Why This Works for MSPs

- **Zero build cost.** No AI-security tooling to acquire, train on, or maintain.
- **Gap in your stack.** Most MSPs manage IT infrastructure. AI is the new attack surface your clients don't know about.
- **Easy sell.** "$500 AI security check-up" is a low-friction add-on to existing managed services agreements.
- **Compliance angle.** EU AI Act (2027-2028), NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 — your clients will need AI readiness assessments. Get in early.
- **Recurring revenue.** Quick Scan → Retainer ($500/mo) → Deep Audit ($2,500). Three-tier upsell ladder.

### Who This Is For

MSPs with 20+ clients who use AI chatbots, AI agents, or RAG pipelines — and don't currently test them. The Okanagan has a growing tech/services sector. If your clients have a chatbot on their website, they have an AI security gap.

### What We Need From You

- Signed white-label agreement (SEB provides template)
- Per-client authorization (client signs our scope form)
- Brand assets for co-branded reports (logo, cover page template)

### What We Don't Do

- No unauthorized testing. Ever. Your client signs first.
- No direct contact with your clients. You own the relationship.
- No competing with you. We do AI security only. You do everything else.
